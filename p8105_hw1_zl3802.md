The mean works for the numeric and logical variables. For the logical
variable, TRUE is treated as 1 and FALSE as 0. The mean does not work
for the character or factor variables.

``` r
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, logical_vector))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, character_vector))
as.numeric(pull(problem2_df, factor_vector))
```

The logical vector becomes 1 and 0. The character vector becomes NA
because the letters cannot be converted to numbers. The factor vector
becomes numeric level codes. This explains why the mean works for
logical variables but not for character or factor variables.
